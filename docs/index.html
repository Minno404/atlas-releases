Your day, handled before you ask.

Every morning Atlas reads you a brief: the weather, your shift, bills due and the news that affects you. Through the day it asks what you need to get done, so tomorrow's brief already knows.

  • Daily brief, or Atlas FM: two hosts read your day like breakfast radio
  • To-dos it collects through the day
  • Reminders, timers, notes and a journal it keeps for you
  • Roster, pay and meal allowance tools for shift work

Your PC and money, watched.

A see-through overlay shows FPS and temperatures over any game. Atlas checks your drives, finds what slows your boot, and reads your bank statements to show where the money goes.

  • Game overlay with FPS, 1% lows, GPU temp and ping
  • Drive health, temperature history and a dust reminder
  • Every subscription and what it costs a year
  • One privacy switch for the mic, camera and screen memory

Questions

Do I need to pay for anything besides Atlas?

You need one AI service. A free Gemini key or offline mode costs nothing. Claude Pro (about A$34 a month) gives the best results and suits the AI dev team best. Everything else Atlas uses is free.

Where is my data kept?

On your PC. Atlas sends only what a task needs to the AI you pick, and one switch turns off the mic, camera and screen memory.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Atlas keeps working on the Basic features. Enter a licence key any time to unlock Plus or Pro; it unlocks at the next start.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11, a microphone for voice, and an internet connection unless you use offline mode.

How do I get a licence key?

Buying opens soon. Until then, the trial has every feature.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, and your data stays on your PC either way.
