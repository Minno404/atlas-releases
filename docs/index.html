Questions

Do I need to pay for anything besides Atlas?

You need one AI service. A free Gemini key or offline mode costs nothing. Claude Pro (about A$34 a month) gives the best results and suits the AI dev team best. Everything else Atlas uses is free.

Where is my data kept?

On your PC. Atlas sends only what a task needs to the AI you pick, and one switch turns off the mic, camera and screen memory.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

Atlas keeps working on the Basic features. Enter a licence key any time to unlock Plus or Pro; it unlocks at the next start.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11, a microphone for voice, and an internet connection unless you use offline mode.

How do I get a licence key?

Buying opens soon. Until then, the trial has every feature.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, and your data stays on your PC either way.
