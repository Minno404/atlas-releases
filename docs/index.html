WHAT IT DOES

Talk to it

Say "Hey Atlas" and talk. It answers out loud in a natural voice, and you can interrupt it.

A holographic HUD

Panels for weather, your day, your games and your projects, in a sci-fi interface you can theme.

Your day, sorted

Morning brief, to-dos, reminders, bills and shifts, and a radio-style show of your day.

A journal it keeps

Atlas writes a notebook about your world, in categories you can correct.

Gaming

A see-through FPS overlay, your game library, lag reports and graphics tuning.

Make games with an AI team

A planner, coder and critic build your game while you sleep, with sounds, music and trailers.

Research and study

Overnight deep dives, podcasts from your files, flashcards, quizzes and skill trees.

Private by design

Everything lives on your PC. One switch turns off the mic, camera and screen memory.

Your phone too

Use Atlas from your phone at home or anywhere, and get alerts when jobs finish.
