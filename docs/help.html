Getting started

  1. Download Atlas and open it. Setup asks your name, your city and what to keep an eye on.
  2. Sign in to Claude with the Claude app, or paste your own Claude or Gemini key in setup.
  3. Say "Hey Atlas, what can you do?" to see this list inside Atlas.
  4. Pair your phone from the panel on the left (Pair phone).
  5. Have a licence key? Paste it in setup, or tell Atlas "here's my licence key".
